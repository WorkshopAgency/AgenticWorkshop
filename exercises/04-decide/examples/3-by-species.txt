A scatter plot from the Palmer penguins dataset. The x-axis is bill length
(mm) and the y-axis is bill depth (mm). It shows the 342 penguins that have
both measurements. Points are coloured by species, with a legend giving the
number of penguins per species: Adelie (n = 151), Chinstrap (n = 68) and
Gentoo (n = 123). A separate straight regression line is fitted for each
species; all three slope upwards, with r = +0.39 (Adelie), +0.65 (Chinstrap)
and +0.64 (Gentoo). The caption reads: "Within each species, penguins with
longer bills have deeper bills. The species differ in average bill shape."
