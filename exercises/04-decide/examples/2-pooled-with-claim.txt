A scatter plot from the Palmer penguins dataset. The x-axis is bill length
(mm) and the y-axis is bill depth (mm). It shows the 342 penguins that have
both measurements. The dataset contains three species (Adelie, Chinstrap and
Gentoo), but all points are drawn in the same grey colour with no legend, so
species cannot be told apart. A single straight regression line is fitted
through all points together; it slopes downwards (r = -0.235). The caption
reads: "Longer bills mean shallower bills in penguins (r = -0.235, n = 342)."
