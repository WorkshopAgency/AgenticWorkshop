## What changed since 2019

The drying protocol was revised after the 2022 calibration study by Dr. Ines
Varrow. Cores are now dried for 48 hours in the drying shed at 40 degrees
Celsius before weighing, replacing the older 36-hour rule. Cores remain 14
centimetres deep.

The Eastern Transect was extended to 1.5 kilometres in 2023 and now has 38
white posts. Counts still start one hour after sunset.

## Safety

The minimum group size on the bog was raised to three people in 2024 after a
volunteer twisted an ankle near post 22. One person in each group carries the
orange radio. The mainland boat now leaves on Mondays and Thursdays at 08:15.

## Equipment

The brass corer was retired in 2023 and replaced by a steel corer with a
depth gauge. Both are stored in the Lamp House. The blue ledger was replaced by
a green ledger in the same year.
